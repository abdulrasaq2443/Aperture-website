Refund policy

Fair and simple refunds.

We want you to be happy with Aperture. You can try it free for 45 days or 15 searches before paying anything.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1

You can get a refund if

  • you were charged twice for the same payment;
  • your payment went through but your access was not activated and we could not fix it within 3 days;
  • you ask within 7 days of your first payment and have not used more than a few searches.
2

Other payments

Once an access period is in use, the payment for it is generally not refundable. Card processing fees charged by Paystack may not be recoverable.

3

How to ask

Email agbajeabdulrasaq03@gmail.com from your account email with the date and amount of the payment. We reply within 3 working days.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Paystack and can take 5 to 10 working days to appear.

4

Cancelling

Plans do not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. Your access simply ends on the date shown in the app.
