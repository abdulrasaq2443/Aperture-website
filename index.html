FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Anything else? Email agbajeabdulrasaq03@gmail.com.

Do my photos leave my phone?

Your originals stay in your phone’s gallery. To describe a photo, the app sends a small, shrunk copy to our secure server. The server analyses it, returns the description and deletes the copy.

Which phones does it work on?

Android first. An iPhone version is planned for later.

Can it read Google Photos?

Not yet. Aperture scans the photos in your phone gallery, which usually includes your backed-up Google Photos. An optional Google Photos import may come after launch.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Search and scanning pause until you choose a plan. Your account stays, and nothing is charged automatically.

A description is wrong. What do I do?

The AI can make mistakes, especially with documents. You will be able to report a wrong description, and those reports help it improve.

How do I get a refund?

Read the refund policy below, then email us from your account address.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Aperture is a smart gallery app run by Abdulrasaq Agbaje, an individual based in Nigeria (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect and why. Contact: agbajeabdulrasaq03@gmail.com.

What we collect
  • Account details: your email address and a password (stored securely by our authentication provider, Supabase).
  • Subscription and payment records: your plan, trial or access dates, and payment references and amounts. Card and bank details are entered on Paystack and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Search usage: a count of searches, so the free trial limit can be applied.
  • Your photos, temporarily: see below.
What happens to your photos

Your original photos stay in your phone’s gallery. To describe a photo, the app shrinks it and sends it over an encrypted connection to our analysis server. The server reads the image (including any text on it), produces a description, keywords and a category, sends these back to your phone, and then deletes the photo. We do not keep your photos and do not use them for advertising.

The description, keywords and a pointer to the photo are saved on your phone. Clearing the app’s data on your phone removes them.

Reporting a wrong description

If you choose to report a wrong description, we save the category, label and your correction as text, with your consent, to improve the app. No photo is sent with a report.

Who we share data with

We use trusted service providers to run Aperture: Supabase (accounts and database), Hugging Face (analysis server hosting), and Paystack (payments). We do not sell your data.

Google Photos

Aperture does not read your Google Photos library. If we add the optional Google Photos Picker in future, it will only receive the photos you select yourself, and this policy will be updated first.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and the records linked to it by emailing us. We will respond within 30 days. We keep payment records for as long as the law and accounting rules require.

Security

Connections are encrypted, access to the analysis server requires a signed-in account, and secrets are kept out of the app. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Children

Aperture is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
