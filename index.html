FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Anything else? Email agbajeabdulrasaq03@gmail.com.

Do my photos leave my phone?

Your originals stay in your phone’s gallery. To describe a photo, the app sends a small, shrunk copy to our secure server. The server analyses it, returns the description and deletes the copy. The text description is saved to your account so it can be restored if you reinstall.

Which phones does it work on?

Android first. An iPhone version is planned for later.

Can it read Google Photos?

Not yet. Aperture scans the photos in your phone gallery, which usually includes your backed-up Google Photos. An optional Google Photos import may come after launch.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Search and new descriptions pause until you choose a plan. Your account stays, and nothing is charged automatically.

A description is wrong. What do I do?

The AI can make mistakes, especially with documents. Open the photo and tap Edit to fix it for yourself, or Report to tell us. Reports help it improve.

What if I delete a photo?

Its description is hidden at once and kept for 30 days in case you restore the photo from your phone’s trash. After that it is deleted from your phone and your account.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and tap “Delete my account”. You can also follow the steps on the account deletion page.

How do I get a refund?

Read the refund policy, then email us from your account address.
