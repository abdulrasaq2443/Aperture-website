Delete your account

Delete your account.

You can delete your Aperture account at any time. This page explains how, and what is removed.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1

Option 1: delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Aperture and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile picture at the top right to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.
2

Option 2: ask us by email

If you cannot open the app, write to agbajeabdulrasaq03@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my account”.

We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

3

What is deleted

  • Your account (email and sign-in).
  • Your saved photo descriptions, keywords, categories, file names and sizes.
  • Your plan and search-count records.
4

What stays

  • Your photos. They are never touched. They stay in your phone’s gallery.
  • Payment records (reference and amount, if you ever paid), only as long as the law and accounting rules require.
  • Face data is stored on your phone only. Remove it in Settings (“Remove my face data”), or by uninstalling the app.
5

Delete only some data

To delete only some data, such as your face data or one photo’s description, you do not need to delete the account. See the privacy policy for details.
